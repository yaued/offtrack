Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

The Short Version

Off Track keeps your trips, leave, memories, saved places, and settings on your iPhone.

There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics tracking. We do not collect or store your data, except for information you choose to send us when contacting support.

Data Stored on Your iPhone

Your leave records, trips, memories, saved places, and app settings are stored locally on your iPhone.

Off Track widgets access a separate copy of the information they need to display content on your Home Screen. This copy remains on your device and is only used by the widgets.

If you back up your iPhone using Apple’s backup services, your Off Track data may be included in that backup.

You can remove your data at any time using Remove All Data in Settings. Deleting the app will also remove the data stored by the app from your device. We do not keep a copy of your data.

Information Sent Outside Your iPhone

Off Track works entirely on your device with the exception of place lookups and external websites.

If you search for a place that is not included in the app’s built-in list, the place name you enter is sent to Wikipedia and Wikivoyage to retrieve information. No trip, leave, memory, or other app data is included in the request.

When you open external links, they are displayed in a browser window within the app. Those websites are operated by third parties and have their own privacy policies and practices.

The Add to Calendar feature only creates an event if you choose to save one.

The “Find trips for” feature is processed entirely on your iPhone.

Feedback and Support

If you choose Contact Us, Off Track prepares an email addressed to offtrackapp@proton.me.

The email may include technical information such as your app version, iOS version, and device model to help diagnose problems. For reports about missing public holidays or regions, it may also include your selected home region and the relevant year.

You can review and edit the email before sending it.

We only receive this information if you send the email. We use it only to respond to your enquiry, investigate issues, and improve the app. The email does not include your trips, leave records, memories, or other personal content unless you choose to add it yourself.

Children

Off Track is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will update this page and revise the “Last updated” date above.
